## Hi there 👋 I'm Zeyu Zhang  

🎓 Data Scientist | Machine Learning Engineer | Applied Researcher  

I recently completed my second Master’s degree in Data Science at the University of Waterloo, where my research focused on financial risk modeling and optimization.

My thesis explored **Probability of Shortfall vs. Expected Shortfall** as risk measures in optimizing Defined Contribution pension plans — combining deep learning, quantitative finance, and stochastic optimization.  
The full research paper is available in this GitHub repository.

Previously, I earned a Master’s degree in Applied Statistics from Beijing Institute of Technology, where I published research in *ACS Omega*, applying machine learning techniques to computational chemistry problems.
